The Problem

Hiring Good English Teachers is Hard & Risky

✕The Pain for Schools & Parents
📄
Resumes lie about English fluency: Almost every teacher writes “Fluent C2”, but in real classrooms they struggle with heavy accents and unclear phrasing.
⏳
Hiring takes 3 to 4 weeks: Schools spend weeks reviewing demo recordings and doing interviews. It costs $800+ just to vet one teacher.
🎓
PDF certificates are faked every day: Traditional TEFL or degree certificates in PDF can be easily edited with no way to verify on the spot.
Bottom Line: Language centers in UAE and Saudi Arabia are desperate to find proven teachers without wasting weeks on bad hires.
✕The Frustration for Good Teachers & AZE
💸
Great teachers are forced to charge cheap: On websites like Preply or Upwork, quality teachers have to compete at $10/hour against unverified tutors.
✂️
Tutor platforms take 33% of earnings: Preply takes the first lesson 100% free, then keeps up to 33% of every class fee, frustrating tutors.
🧩
AZE stops at student test fees ($15): We already have the best speech and grading AI, but we don’t connect those scores to hiring managers who pay $200+/month!
The Opportunity: If AZE gives teachers a verified badge and a direct hiring link, both teachers and schools will happily pay us.
The Solution

4 Simple Steps: How This Platform Works
